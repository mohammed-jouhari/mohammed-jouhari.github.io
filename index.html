About

World’s Top 2% Scientist · Stanford / Elsevier, 2026 ↗

I am an Assistant Professor in Computer Science at the Faculty of Law and Political Science, Ibn Tofail University in Kenitra, Morocco. I obtained my PhD there in 2019, working on underwater wireless sensor networks — MAC layer optimization, topology control and geographic routing.

I then held postdoctoral positions at Qatar University and Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (UM6P), on AI-driven surveillance and energy-efficient IoT networks. My current work targets intrusion detection for IoT with deep learning, federated learning for industrial cybersecurity, and scalable LoRaWAN architectures.

I serve the community through editorial work, peer review (55+ for IEEE journals) and programme-committee membership at major IEEE conferences.
